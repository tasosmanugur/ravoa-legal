What Ravoa provides

Ravoa is a personal travel-record and progress-tracking app. It lets you record countries, destinations, journeys, stays and experiences, then presents your Passport, statistics and achievements. It is not a booking service, official travel document, verified travel credential, navigation service or travel-safety advisory.

Ravoa is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It currently has no age-verification system. No Ravoa account is required. There are no in-app ads, free trials or monthly subscription plans in the launch offering.

Free features and Premium

Free features include your Passport, creating/editing/deleting trips, recording experiences, the six Stats Overview metrics, and the 21 free achievements. The app currently has no Premium limit on the number of trips you can record.

Premium provides advanced Travel Patterns and Records statistics and access to the nine Premium achievements. The paywall also lets free users preview Solo Travel Rate and Average Trip Length calculated from their own trips. That limited preview does not unlock the full advanced Stats screen.

Buying Premium does not automatically earn an achievement. Each achievement's travel/experience conditions must also be met. Statistics and progress are recalculated when you change or delete records. Premium achievements can become locked if the relevant conditions are no longer met or Premium access ends; the underlying local travel records are not deleted for that reason.

Entries are self-reported and calculations depend on the data available. For example, missing hostel names affect unique-hostel progress, while hostel stays still count. The app does not independently verify that travel took place.

Purchase restoration does not restore your travel journal. It does not recover trips, profile data, experience answers or other records removed from the device. Ravoa has no travel-data cloud backup or sign-in account that restores those records.
