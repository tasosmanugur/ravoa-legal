Ravoa: Backpacker Passport is a personal travel journal and progress tracker. Record your journeys, collect experiences and build your Backpacker Passport.

The initial App Store launch is limited to the United States, Australia and New Zealand.

Contact Ravoa

Osman Ugur Tas, operating as Ravoa, is the individual developer based in Türkiye. Email him directly at ravoa.support@gmail.com for help with the app or purchases, to exercise a privacy right or to make a privacy complaint.

Please describe the issue and, if useful, include your app version and iOS version. You can find the app version under Me. Only send screenshots or purchase details that are needed to explain the problem. Please do not send your Apple Account password, full payment-card details or unrelated travel records.

Restore a purchase

  • Open Ravoa and go to Me.
  • Tap Backpacker Passport Premium, or Premium Active if access is already recognised.
  • Choose Restore Purchases on the Premium screen. Connect to the internet and use the Apple Account associated with the purchase.
  • If access is still missing, contact us before buying again.

Restoring a purchase checks your Apple purchase entitlement. It does not restore trips, your profile or other travel records. Ravoa does not provide a cloud travel-data backup or a Ravoa sign-in account.

Manage an Annual subscription

If Ravoa recognises your active Annual plan, open Me → Premium Active → Manage Subscription to open Apple's subscription controls.

You can also manage the plan in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Ravoa. Follow Apple's cancellation instructions if you cannot find the subscription.

Cancelling automatic renewal normally leaves access available until the end of the paid period. Refunds and any different cancellation rights required by local law are separate. Uninstalling Ravoa, resetting app data or emailing a privacy-deletion request does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Lifetime Access

Lifetime Premium is a single purchase with no recurring subscription charge. The membership screen shows Lifetime Access when the purchase is recognised. It does not show a subscription-management option for Lifetime.

If Lifetime access is missing, try Restore Purchases. The Terms of Use explain its scope and the distinction between ongoing Premium access and a guarantee that the app will operate forever.

Billing or refund help

Apple processes App Store purchases. You can request a refund through Apple. Contact us if you need help identifying an app problem or exercising a right against the app provider. Directing you to Apple does not remove any mandatory consumer rights you have.

Your travel records and reset

Your profile and travel records are stored on your device. You can edit your profile, change experience answers, and edit or delete individual trips in the app.

Me → Reset App Data removes the app's saved local profile, trips, answers and progress. It does not delete Apple or RevenueCat purchase history, cancel an active subscription, or request deletion of third-party records. Restore Purchases will not recover the travel records you remove.

Do not rely on reinstalling the app or a device backup to recover travel records. See the Privacy Policy for storage, cache and deletion details.

Geographic data credits

Ravoa contains modified GeoNames countryInfo and cities5000 data under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Changes include country filtering, reduced fields and display-name compatibility adjustments. Backpacker destination additions are maintained by Ravoa. Destination searches use bundled data; the app does not send your searches to GeoNames.

Globe outlines use public-domain data from Natural Earth.
