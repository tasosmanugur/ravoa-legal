This policy describes personal information handled in Ravoa: Backpacker Passport, through support, and on this support website. Local travel records and purchase-related data are handled differently. Your travel journal stays on your device; Apple and RevenueCat process information needed for the purchase and Premium-access system, along with technical information described below.

Who is responsible

Osman Ugur Tas, operating as Ravoa, is the individual developer based in Türkiye and the data controller for information processed for Ravoa’s purposes. He is your contact for privacy questions, requests to exercise your rights and complaints. You can reach him directly at ravoa.support@gmail.com.

This policy is prepared for the initial launch in the United States, Australia and New Zealand. Rights depend on the law that applies to you and to the processing involved.

Information stored on your device

Ravoa stores the following information locally to provide your Passport and travel history:

  • Profile: the name you enter and your optional home country.
  • Onboarding: completion status, selected countries and cities/destinations, including custom place names.
  • Experiences: your Yes/Not Yet answers. Relevant trip records can also provide evidence that an experience is complete.
  • Trips: a local record ID, trip name, start and end dates, solo/with-others travel style, and destinations in the order you enter them.
  • Stays: destination city and country, stay type, and, for hostels, an optional hostel name and dorm/private room type.

The app calculates visited places, nights, hostel stays, statistics, XP, level, traveller title and achievement progress from those records. Premium access is checked separately using RevenueCat. The two personalised statistics on the paywall are calculated on the device from your own trips; they are not uploaded as analytics events.

Your home country is profile information; setting it does not automatically mark that country as visited. Search text, unsaved form entries and screen selections are generally temporary. Local graphics and system caches can also contain derived information, such as the countries coloured on your globe.

The current app does not send your profile name, home country, trip history, hostel names or experience answers to Ravoa's own server or to RevenueCat. Ravoa has no travel-data server, cloud sync or user-account system. If you include any of those details in a support email, they will be processed as support information instead.

Apple and RevenueCat purchase information

We use Apple StoreKit and RevenueCat, Inc. to load Premium products, validate purchases, restore access and check whether Premium is active. RevenueCat also provides purchase/subscription reporting.

Ravoa checks a separate on-device privacy-notice record before starting the RevenueCat SDK.

  • New users: the information appears on Welcome. The SDK does not start until you tap Build my Passport and the notice version is saved.
  • Existing users who have completed onboarding: if there is no current notice record, Ravoa shows the information with a Continue button without restarting onboarding. This does not undo processing that may already have occurred in an earlier app version.
  • Later app launches: once the saved current notice version has loaded, the SDK can start automatically without showing the same notice again. It can refresh when the app becomes active. A missing, outdated or unrecognised record causes the notice to be shown again.

After you continue, RevenueCat can connect while onboarding is still in progress and even if you never buy Premium. Opening the Privacy Policy link and returning to the app does not count as continuing. If the required notice record cannot be read or saved, SDK startup stays blocked and the app offers a retry.

The local notice record contains only a version number. It is separate from your travel records and is not cleared by Reset App Data. Continuing is not recorded as consent or acceptance of the Terms of Use. The app currently has no separate purchase-analytics opt-out.

The purchase system processes these categories:

  • A RevenueCat App User ID: the SDK generates a random anonymous ID and caches it on the device. We do not set it to your profile name or email. It still links purchase-related records and is not a guarantee of legally anonymous data.
  • Purchase and access records: product and transaction identifiers, receipt or signed-transaction information, purchase/renewal/expiry information, and Premium entitlement status. RevenueCat returns customer information that Ravoa uses to determine access.
  • Technical information: device model, operating system, app and SDK versions, app/bundle information, language/locale, storefront and connection-related information. The SDK sends Apple's identifier for vendor (IDFV) in a request header when the system supplies it. This transmission is separate from explicitly setting a RevenueCat customer attribute.
  • Tracking-permission status: the SDK can read and synchronise the device's existing App Tracking Transparency status. Reading that status is not a request for tracking permission and does not mean you have authorised tracking.
  • Network and activity information: IP address in connection handling, first/last connection information, and purchase-related activity. RevenueCat describes using transaction country, or country inferred from IP when needed, in its customer records. Its documentation says the IP used to determine that country is then discarded; that does not establish a retention rule for every infrastructure or security log.

Apple handles the payment method and Apple Account used for checkout. Ravoa does not receive your full payment-card details or Apple Account password. Apple explains its own processing in App Store & Privacy. For information on the purchase records RevenueCat maintains for app developers, see its customer-record documentation.

Purposes of processing

The purposes are to provide your local travel journal and progress calculations; supply and verify Premium access; administer purchase restoration and subscription status; understand purchase/subscription activity through RevenueCat; address support and privacy requests; and meet applicable legal obligations.

Processing necessary to fulfil a Premium purchase or restoration request is distinct from purchase analytics and SDK activity before a purchase. A contractual basis for requested purchase functionality does not automatically cover those other activities. Opening Ravoa or accepting its Terms does not, by itself, provide consent where a separate valid consent is required.

Recipients and international processing

RevenueCat processes purchase-related and technical information to verify purchases, check Premium access and provide purchase reports to Ravoa. Apple handles App Store transactions and its own store-related processing. We also receive information you voluntarily send to support; the current Gmail address uses Google's email service. We may need to disclose information in response to a valid legal requirement or to handle a legal claim, subject to applicable law.

The provider is based in Türkiye. RevenueCat is based in the United States. Its published Data Processing Addendum lists subprocessors and locations, including US infrastructure, logging and analytics providers, and sets out contractual provisions for qualifying international transfers. The existence of those published terms is not a guarantee that every transfer is covered by the same legal mechanism. Apple, Google and the website host may also process information outside your country. These external services do not receive the app's local travel journal merely because you use them for purchases or visit this website. Contact us if you need information about the recipients or safeguards relevant to your data.

Retention and deletion

Local records: saved profile, travel and experience records remain on your device until you change or remove them, reset the app, or remove the app's data. There is no automatic expiry period. Search/form state and graphics caches have separate lifecycles; Reset App Data is not a secure-erasure tool for every operating-system cache or pre-existing backup.

Purchases: Apple and RevenueCat records are separate from the local travel journal. Resetting or uninstalling Ravoa does not cancel a subscription or instruct those providers to erase purchase records. Restoring access can associate an Apple purchase with a RevenueCat customer record again. A RevenueCat deletion request does not itself delete Apple's records or cancel billing.

RevenueCat documents a separate, asynchronous customer-deletion process. Its published Data Processing Addendum addresses the return or deletion of records within its scope when the developer’s service agreement ends, subject to backup and legal exceptions. Cancelling your Premium plan does not start that process. To request deletion of your Ravoa purchase-related information, contact Osman as described below. The time it takes to remove a customer profile can differ from the retention period for backups.

Support and privacy requests: the support mailbox receives your email address, message and any attachments you send. How long this information is needed depends on whether your question is still open, whether there is a specific dispute and whether the law requires a record of the request and response. Verification materials are needed only to establish who may receive the information or authorise the action, unless a specific legal duty requires their retention. You can ask Osman to delete support correspondence using the email above. Reset App Data does not clear the mailbox or its attachments.

Your local controls and backups

  • Edit: use Me to edit your profile; use Trips to edit or delete a journey; use Experiences to change manual answers.
  • Reset: Me → Reset App Data removes the saved local profile, trips, answers and progress. It does not remove Apple or RevenueCat purchase history or cancel an active subscription.
  • Uninstall: deleting the app normally removes its local app data. Apple's Offload feature is different and can retain documents and data.
  • Restore Purchases: this restores eligible purchase access, not trips, profile details or experience history.

Ravoa does not provide a cloud backup or cross-device travel sync. The current local-storage implementation excludes its storage directory from iCloud backup by default. Device migration, old backups and system caches have not been verified as a recovery method; please do not rely on them to recover deleted travel records. There is currently no built-in travel-data export.

Your privacy rights

Email ravoa.support@gmail.com with a description of your request and, where relevant, your country or state/province. Please do not send passwords, full card details or identity documents with an initial request.

Depending on the applicable law, rights may include access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, portability, withdrawal of consent where consent is used, and complaint or appeal rights. These rights have legal limits. The local travel journal is not available to us remotely; we can explain the on-device controls and separately address information held through support or the purchase system.

A profile name alone does not identify a RevenueCat record. We may need limited information to locate the right record and verify a request before disclosing or deleting it. There is no public support-ID control in the current app. We do not promise to retrieve your travel history from a server that does not exist.

Information for the launch markets

United States: applicable state rights can differ and may include access, correction, deletion, a copy of information, appeal and protection from discrimination for exercising a right. Ravoa has no in-app ads or advertising-targeting implementation.

Australia: where the Privacy Act and Australian Privacy Principles apply, you can seek access or correction and raise privacy complaints, including with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

New Zealand: where the Privacy Act 2020 applies, you can seek access and correction and complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Permissions, advertising and children

The current app does not request GPS, camera, photo-library, microphone, contact or notification access. Country and city entries describe travel you record, not live GPS tracking. Notifications is not an active notification service.

There are no in-app ads, advertising-ID collection calls or separate app analytics/crash-reporting services configured in the app code. RevenueCat purchase reporting and technical processing, and Apple's own store/diagnostic practices, are separate from that statement. The current app does not request ATT permission.

Ravoa is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It does not verify age or technically prevent a younger person from opening it. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Ravoa's external services or support, contact us so the situation can be assessed under applicable law.

Security

Local records are kept in the app's device storage. Ravoa does not add its own encrypted journal/database layer. Device security and the safeguards of Apple, RevenueCat and other relevant providers also matter. No storage or transmission system can be described as completely risk-free. Protect access to your device and send only the information needed when contacting support.

This website and external links

This is a static support and legal site with no sign-in, contact form, advertising scripts, visitor analytics, external font loading or application-set cookies/local storage. Email links open your chosen email application; they do not automatically send a message.

When you visit these pages on GitHub Pages, GitHub handles the hosting request. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including for visitors who are not signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Following an external link takes you to that provider's service and privacy practices. GeoNames data in the app is bundled offline; the app does not make live destination-search requests to GeoNames. Geographic data credits are available on the Support page.
